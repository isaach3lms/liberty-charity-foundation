The idea

Liberty is not something given. It is something built through compassion in action.

A community living in liberty is not simply free in a legal sense. It is a place where people have real capacity to move forward: access to opportunity, freedom from preventable barriers, and the dignity to make choices that shape their own future.

That capacity has to be built. It is built in a specific order, and each stage makes the next one possible.
